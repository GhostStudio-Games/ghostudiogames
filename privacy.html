Master Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 27, 2026
Last Updated: July 27, 2026

This Master Privacy Policy applies to ghoSTudio games ("we," "us," or "our"), our website (https://ghostudiogames.com), and all mobile and PC games published by ghoSTudio games on platforms including the Google Play Store and Steam (collectively, the "Services").

By accessing or playing any of our games or using our website, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Scope & Application Across Our Titles

Because our games offer different features, the level of data collection depends on the specific game you play:

  • Ad-Free & Offline Titles: Games offered without advertisements or in-app purchases process minimal to zero data locally on your device.
  • Ad-Supported Titles: Games displaying banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads use third-party ad networks that may process anonymized device identifiers to serve contextually relevant ads.
  • In-App Purchase (IAP) Enabled Titles: Games that offer digital purchases (e.g., coin packs, ad removal) utilize platform billing services (such as Google Play Billing or Steam) to process transactions safely.
  • PC & Steam Titles: Games published on Steam may integrate Steamworks features, such as cloud saves, leaderboards, and achievements tied to your Steam Account ID.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the title and platform, we and our service providers may process the following types of information:

A. Automatically Collected Technical Data

  • Device Identifiers: Mobile Advertising ID (e.g., Google Advertising ID / GAID), IP address, device model, operating system version, mobile network information, language, and system performance metrics.
  • Gameplay & Usage Data: High scores, level progression, game achievements, crash logs, and session statistics.

B. Payment & Transaction Information

  • For games featuring In-App Purchases, all financial transactions are handled directly and securely by platform store providers (e.g., Google Play, Valve / Steam). ghoSTudio games does not collect, access, or store your credit/debit card details, bank credentials, or full billing address. We only receive confirmation receipts to deliver digital items to your game account.

C. Direct Communications

  • If you contact us directly for customer support or feedback, we collect your email address and any details you voluntarily include in your message.

3. How We Use Collected Information

We use processed information solely to:

  • Deliver core gameplay functionality, cloud saves, and achievement tracking.
  • Facilitate and restore digital in-app purchases (such as virtual currency or ad-removal passes).
  • Serve, optimize, and measure non-intrusive or targeted advertisements in supported titles.
  • Diagnose technical errors, prevent exploit/fraud activity, and fix performance bugs.
  • Respond to direct support queries or feedback sent by players.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our Services are designed for general audiences and are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or request personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us at gamesghostudio@gmail.com, and we will promptly delete the data.

6. User Ad Tracking & Data Choice

You have full control over interest-based advertising on your mobile device:

  • Android Devices: Open Settings > Google > Ads > tap Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • In-Game Options: Where available, you can opt for ad-removal options (e.g., "No Ads" purchases) or decline personalized consent prompts.

7. Data Retention & Security

We take reasonable technical measures to safeguard any data processed through our third-party SDK partners. Data collected via automated analytics is generally retained in aggregated, non-personally identifiable formats according to the retention schedules of our SDK partners.
